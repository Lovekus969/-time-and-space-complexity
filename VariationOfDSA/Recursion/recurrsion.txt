here we will see the concepts of recurssion and see what is recursion why we use it and why it works . 

Recursion is when a function calls itself—directly or indirectly—to solve a problem.

The key idea is:
          
          You break a big problem into smaller versions of the same problem.
          Eventually, you reach a base case (a condition where you stop calling the function again).

Structure of Recursion:
                                             
          Base case – the simplest version of the problem that can be solved without further calls.
          Recursive case – where the function calls itself with a smaller or simpler input.
